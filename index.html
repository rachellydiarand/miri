This is the style frame I had in the 1990's. A "tri-fold" frame. The advantage with it is that it is low to the ground. The frame keeps the futon off of the ground so that it gets air circulation on the bottom. When it is low to the ground, you can easy put things on the ground next to it to get to them. Something that is on the ground cannot fall further. It can tip over, but breaks are less common with a shorter fall. And it's easier to put a short to the ground table there.

I had it in my meditation studio.

I'd gladly trade the frame I have now for one of these but I think I had problems selling mine and there are none of these for free on Craigslist right now.
